The latest data

Rates that compare countries fairly arrive about two and a half years late: every death needs a certified cause. A few series move within the year.

They are provisional and revised as records are completed, so they show direction, not final levels. More countries will be added as their licences are confirmed.

Latest national figures: suicide

Newest annual figure from each country's own statistics office, where it is ahead of the comparable Eurostat year

National figures use each country's own definitions and rate types, so they are not ranked against each other; positions on the dashboard use the comparable Eurostat figures. "Calculated" rates are deaths divided by population from the same statistics office, computed by our build script; where an office publishes counts only, no rate is shown.
