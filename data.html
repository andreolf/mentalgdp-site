Data & sources

Every number has an address

Figures are pulled by script from public APIs and stored exactly as received. Nobody retypes a value. Any number on this site can be traced to the file and request that produced it.

01

How it works

Three steps, all by script, so every figure can be reproduced.

Fetch

From the publisher's API

One script per source requests the data and saves the raw response with the URL and time of the request.

Check

Against the licence

Only sources with an open licence verdict are published. The rest are listed with what is needed to use them.

Build

Into the dashboard

A build script reads the stored files and writes the data behind this site. Re-running it reproduces every figure.

Indicators on the dashboard

Each indicator below comes from an open-licence source, with the exact query and the date it was fetched.

03

What we may use, and what is pending

Three findings shape the index. The only global source of depression and anxiety prevalence (IHME) is non-commercial. WHO's dataset terms bar use that promotes a commercial enterprise, which also covers WHO figures relayed by the World Bank and UN. The Legatum Prosperity Index, the basis of the 2020 version, cannot be reused without written consent.

Verdicts were checked in October 2026 and are reviewed before every release. Licence wording is confirmed by a person before publication.

04

Gaps we are working on

Most inputs are annual and two to three years old. Health interview surveys run every five to seven years. National statistics offices and health ministries have now been reviewed for all 33 focus jurisdictions.

Year latest year loaded, within five years Year latest year loaded, older – no open data loaded Found in-year series identified, not yet loaded
United Kingdom

Eurostat series for the UK end between 2014 and 2019. National sources are rich but split by nation: ONS for England and Wales, NRS for Scotland, NISRA for Northern Ireland, and NHS England monthly service statistics for England only.

Switzerland

Covered by Eurostat for suicide, workforce, beds and life satisfaction, but not by the EU health interview survey. Assisted suicide is reported separately from suicide.

United States

Monthly and quarterly provisional mortality from the CDC. Survey prevalence is annual since the Household Pulse mental health items stopped in September 2024.

Israel, China and Brazil

No open, comparable source is loaded yet. China has no official API or open licence; Brazil's Ministry of Health data carry a no-derivatives licence that needs a legal read.
